What S3 Storage Costs for a CAD Vault

s3pdm works with any S3-compatible storage. Here's what ten providers charge, what you can get for free, and what your vault would cost each month.

    Keep client work off IPFS

    Filebase stores files on IPFS, a public network. A "private" bucket only hides the S3 address: anyone who has or finds a file's content ID can download it. That's fine for public files, but not for confidential designs.

    Why Wasabi's 90-day rule barely matters here

    s3pdm never deletes file contents from storage, so the 90-day minimum only touches its small history files. The 1 TB minimum is what counts: below 1 TB, you pay for 1 TB.

    Storage You Already Own

    s3pdm can also store a vault in a folder on a network drive or NAS, or in a self-hosted S3 server such as Garage or MinIO, with no storage fees at all. It's worth considering if you already run a server, though your data is then only as safe as that hardware and its backups.
